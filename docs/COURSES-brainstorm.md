# Course ideas (fill these in from Admin → Courses)

The six drafts are already seeded as **Coming soon**, with no prices. Everything below is a suggestion; set your own prices, durations and tutors. Mode of teaching can be Online, Physical or Hybrid.

## Track: Building with AI
| Course | Who it is for | Suggested outcomes | Suggested format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Certified Prompt Engineering** | Anyone who uses AI tools at work or school | Write structured prompts, reuse templates, check AI answers, a short assessment for the certificate | Short course, a few weeks, mostly live online |
| **AI for Startups** | Founders and small business owners | Use AI for market research, content, customer support and simple automation; a one-page AI plan for their business | Workshop style, practical, online or hybrid |
| **AI for Kids** | Ages roughly 8–15 (parent registers) | What AI is, safe use, simple creative projects, an end-of-course showcase | Physical or hybrid, short sessions, parent and child rules |
| **AI for Programming: Web Development** | Beginners and junior developers | HTML/CSS/JavaScript with AI-assisted coding, build and publish a small site | Longer course, project based |
| **AI for Programming: Mobile App Development** | Beginners and junior developers | Build a simple mobile app with AI-assisted coding | Longer course, project based; needs web basics first |

## Track: Cybersecurity

|---|---|---|
| **Cybersecurity** (intro) | Staff, students, small businesses | Passwords and accounts, phishing, safe devices and networks, basic incident response. You can later split it into "Cybersecurity Awareness" and "Practical Security" |

## Tips for the admin form
- **Description:** two or three sentences on what the student will be able to do afterwards.
- **Rules:** attendance, assignments, code of conduct, certificate conditions. These show after registering.
- **Payment plan:** e.g. deposit, instalments and deadlines. Put the till number once in Admin → Pages → Academy.
- Set **Status = Open** only when you are ready to take students.
